About Us

Local Pest Control You Can Call Directly

From the red clay of the Triangle to the coastal sands of Brunswick County, The Bug Guys protect North Carolina homes with 45 years of combined experience. We're family-owned and locally operated, and we've spent decades learning which pests show up in every season and why.

There are no call centers and no rotating technicians who don't know your property. You get a team of neighbors and a direct line to the owner. We use EPA-approved products that are tough on bugs but safe for your kids, pets, and the North Carolina environment, and if the bugs come back between scheduled visits, so do we, at no additional cost.
